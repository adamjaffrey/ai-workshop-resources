# System Blueprint

## Objective

Draft emails for me so I don't have to, saving me time.

## Future-State Workflow

🟩 check email at 9 am
↓
🟪 look at only new emails
↓
🟪 filter out newsletters
↓
🟪 draft replies
↓
🟦 user reviews
↓
🟨 user sends
↓
🟩 all emails responded to

## Build Scope

- 🟪 look at only new emails
- 🟪 filter out newsletters
- 🟪 draft replies

## Required Inputs

- 🟪 look at only new emails: my inbox.
- 🟪 filter out newsletters: what looks like a newsletter versus an email from a real person.
- 🟪 draft replies: the emails and my `BUSINESS.md` file.

## Required Behaviour

1. 🟪 look at only new emails: find unread emails since the last run.
2. 🟪 filter out newsletters: decide how to filter for real emails.
3. 🟪 draft replies: draft the reply in my voice and according to `BUSINESS.md`.

## Expected Outputs

1. 🟪 look at only new emails: a list of new emails.
2. 🟪 filter out newsletters: a filtered list.
3. 🟪 draft replies: all new emails with drafts written.

Immediate hand-off: 🟦 user reviews.

## Critical Rules

Only draft, never send.

## Human Oversight

I will review the emails and manually send them.

## Definition of Done

### How will we know when we're done?

- All new emails from real people have drafts written.
- The drafts match my tone of voice.
